Stores: {{ if .Values.stores.garage.enabled }}{{ .Values.stores.garage.name }} (Garage) {{ end }}{{ range .Values.stores.external }}{{ .name }} (S3) {{ end }}{{ range .Values.stores.mirrors }}{{ .name }} (mirror) {{ end }}
They are registered with the service by the sidecar of Garage and by the Job cinim-storage-register. See them (the image has no shell: use a port-forward and curl):
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ .Values.blobgate.fullnameOverride }} {{ .Values.blobgate.config.port }}
  curl -H "Authorization: Bearer <admin token>" http://127.0.0.1:{{ .Values.blobgate.config.port }}/v1/admin/stores
An organisation chooses its store with PUT /api/v1/organizations/<slug>/storage on the core (docs/artifacts.md).
